# Public governance summary

This is the minimal public record needed to understand the authorized scope of
FilmSimulation. The private governance repository, discussions, identities,
comments, and unrelated records are not part of the curated public snapshot.

## Decision 0004 — Public-data external comparison

Recorded 2026-11-20.

- Authorized Phase P6 to search, classify, and compare public data only where
  the quantities and provenance are scientifically comparable.
- Allowed `unavailable` and `not_evaluable` outcomes when documented.
- Did not authorize physical-film measurement, equipment or film purchases,
  commercial LUT purchases, paid datasets, or promotion of model-derived data
  to independent physical validation.
- Kept P6 independent of Phase P5 closure and preserved every existing
  evidence-level distinction.

## Decision 0006 — Status reconciliation

Recorded 2026-11-25.

- Accepted `FS-P5-03`, `FS-P6-01`, and `FS-P6-02` as `completed` on the basis
  of their existing evidence and QC records.
- Kept `FS-P5-02` **blocked** because no fair comparable 5219-to-2383 LUT is
  available.
- Preserved the existing scientific acceptance criteria, `not_evaluable`
  results, G4 limitations, frozen research evidence, and the boundary between
  software/model checks and physical-film validation.
- Did not authorize a broader research roadmap or any claim of experimentally
  verified physical-film fidelity.

## Current public boundary

FilmSimulation remains an offline computational research prototype. A physical
laboratory dataset is not required for its published-reference numerical gate;
missing independent physical measurements remain explicit limitations. This
summary authorizes no repository merge, publication, or visibility change.
